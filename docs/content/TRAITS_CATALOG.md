## Authoring constraints

- Traits are binary.
- Oppositions are symmetric.
- No automatic permanent Stat bonus is implied by a Trait.
- Traits affect Events/Choices/Dice only when content explicitly queries them.
- Every Trait should remain reusable across at least five distinct Event contexts.
