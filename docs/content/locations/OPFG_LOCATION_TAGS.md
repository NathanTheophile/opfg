# OPFG Location Tags

> Status: **validated specialized content authority**.

Controlled vocabulary for mass Event authoring. Tags are intentionally limited; location `type`, `seaId` and hierarchy carry information that should not be duplicated with ad-hoc tags.

## Rules

- Use only tags from this document.
- Prefer 0–5 strong tags over exhaustive description.
- A tag describes a recurring Event-writing constraint/opportunity, not trivia.
- Do not infer faction presence from a single historical visit.
- `coastal` does not automatically imply `port`; `port` does not automatically imply `shipyard`.
- `shipMarket` is runtime/authoring data and must not be inferred merely from `port` or `trade`; use the validated `none | small_craft | full` rules from the [Content Bible](../CONTENT_BIBLE.md).
- Environmental tags should describe a defining local condition, not a one-off scene.

## Vocabulary

### `capital`
Administrative or political capital; use only when the location is explicitly a capital.

### `city`
Urban settlement large enough to support varied civilian events.

### `village`
Small settlement suited to local/rural life events.

### `coastal`
Directly connected to a coast or normal maritime access.

### `port`
Location with explicit port/harbor function.

### `trade`
Strong fit for merchants, cargo, markets or transient crews.

### `shipyard`
Ship construction/repair is a defining local activity.

### `marine_presence`
Marine authority, base or regular Marine activity is narratively relevant.

### `pirate_presence`
Pirates are a persistent or defining local factor.

### `revolutionary_presence`
Revolutionary Army presence is a defining local factor.

### `government`
World Government or state administration is a defining factor.

### `royal`
Monarchy, royal court or noble rule is directly relevant.

### `wealthy`
Affluent environment where wealth/class can shape events.

### `poor`
Poverty or deprived living conditions are a defining local factor.

### `industrial`
Industry, factories, heavy production or engineering are defining.

### `agricultural`
Farming or agricultural production is defining.

### `criminal`
Organized crime, lawlessness or illicit activity is structurally relevant.

### `military`
Military infrastructure, operations or hierarchy are defining.

### `prison`
Detention/incarceration is a defining function.

### `research`
Science, laboratories or experimentation are defining.

### `medical`
Medicine, hospitals or medical culture are strongly relevant.

### `historic`
The place has strong historical/event-writing significance.

### `religious`
Religion, worship or sacred institutions are defining.

### `entertainment`
Entertainment venues or spectacle are a major local activity.

### `touristic`
Visitors/tourism are a normal source of local activity.

### `dangerous`
Routine presence there implies elevated physical or narrative danger.

### `isolated`
Meaningfully cut off from normal travel or information flow.

### `urban`
Dense built environment.

### `rural`
Low-density settlement/countryside context.

### `desert`
Desert environment is defining.

### `forest`
Forest environment is defining.

### `mountain`
Mountainous terrain is defining.

### `snow`
Snow/cold climate is defining.

### `tropical`
Tropical climate is defining.

### `wilderness`
Non-urban natural environment is the main context.

### `sky`
Sky-island/sky-sea geography.

### `underwater`
Underwater/deep-sea geography.
