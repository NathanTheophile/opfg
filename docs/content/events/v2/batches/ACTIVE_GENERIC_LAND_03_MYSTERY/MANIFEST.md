## Scope

Generic on-land Active mysteries: tampered locks and ledgers, false notices, contradictory witnesses, hidden passages, swapped cargo evidence, coded marks, false identities, suspicious absences and same-scene investigations. Mysteries close or reach a concrete immediate resolution inside the monthly root + Immediate chain.

Explicitly excluded: ordinary commerce, direct combat/riot/threat framing, supernatural spectacle as primary hook, Scheduled callbacks, Lifetime Threads, exact-destination travel, new persistent definitions.
