## Regional Lifetime — Coastwatch Bell

Intended span: roughly **9.5 years** after the seed.

```text
coastwatch_bell
  -> Immediate: broken_clapper
      ├─ +12m lt02_watch ──┐
      └─ +12m lt02_books ──┴─ +18m lt03_storm
                               ├─ +24m lt04_open_chain ─┐
                               └─ +24m lt04_paid_chain ─┴─ +24m lt05_reunion
                                                            └─ +36m lt06_legacy
```

- Durable anchor: East Blue coastal warning-bell network.
- Branching: repair/watch vs audit/books, then open/public vs subscription-funded network; deliberate reconvergence between splits.
- No MajorTrack metadata, ArcState, quest state or custom progress counter.
- All progression is Scheduled + History-derived.
- Returns are letters/gazettes and `scheduledReach: unrestricted`, so the Lifetime can continue after the player leaves East Blue without teleporting the player back.
- The Lifetime seed is limited to `ageMonths <= 306`, leaving its longest ~114-month path room to resolve before the 420-month V1 horizon.
