# ACTIVE_HAKI_ARMAMENT_V1 — NARRATIVE BLUEPRINT

## Thread — Les cinq impossibles

The five mini-adventures share one principle: **Armament is not simply hitting harder. It is learning what must yield — your body, your defense, your weapon, or the obstacle in front of you.** Each arc reuses the image of a chosen contact line: first a point on a shell, then a guarded surface, a structural weakness, a timing window, and finally a fracture line inside an impossible impact.

## Level I — La chose qu’on ne peut pas frapper

- **Premise:** on land, an armored boar overturns a cart and threatens wounded travelers. Ordinary impacts rebound uselessly from its shell.
- **Escalation role:** awakening; the player discovers the first instinctive Armament coating while protecting someone from a final charge.
- **Beat sheet:**
  1. Root — the armored boar overturns a cart and pins a porter.
  2. i01 — repeated blows rebound; the shell “answers” every impact.
  3. i02 — the trapped porter must be freed while the beast circles.
  4. i03 — the player learns angle and timing matter more than raw force.
  5. i04 — the charge line shifts toward a shelter holding wounded travelers.
  6. i05 — an iron bar bends uselessly; tools stop being the answer.
  7. i06 — the player deliberately stands between the charge and the wounded.
  8. i07 — the world narrows to one chosen contact point.
  9. i08 — the first dark coating manifests and the shell finally cracks.
- **Mastered:** first instinctive manifestation; Armament can make the body refuse an impact and impose force externally.
- **Choice themes:** protection, angle, sacrifice, refusing to retreat, rescuing before fighting.
- **Risk:** low/moderate; real Health loss is possible, but the arc is not designed as a death trap.
- **Secondary rewards:** small Morale/Luck/Charisma gains and Reputation for protecting others.
- **Thread connection:** establishes the first “line that must yield.”

## Level II — La peau contre l’acier

- **Premise:** at sea aboard the player’s ship, a torn winch sends a steel cable scything across the deck and trapping a traveler.
- **Escalation role:** control; deliberate Armament becomes a defensive guard rather than a lucky offensive flash.
- **Beat sheet:**
  1. Root — the winch tears loose and the cable lashes the deck.
  2. i01 — the steel hook returns in a tightening rhythm.
  3. i02 — the trapped traveler must be freed without tightening the cable.
  4. i03 — a passenger is threatened; bare hands become the only shield.
  5. i04 — the whole winch threatens to tear free.
  6. i05 — the player must hold a vibrating steel line under tension.
  7. i06 — deliberate guarding replaces instinctive hardening.
  8. i07 — the player maintains Armament while forcing a bent steel pin.
  9. i08 — steel strikes a consciously blackened forearm and loses.
- **Mastered:** deliberate defensive coating, selective coverage, and intentional release.
- **Choice themes:** intercept, protect, stabilize, command, use the deck and rigging intelligently.
- **Risk:** moderate; cable impacts can cause substantial Health loss and minor resource damage.
- **Secondary rewards:** Navigation/Charisma/Morale, Reputation, small material costs.
- **Thread connection:** the “yield line” moves from the enemy surface to the player’s own chosen guard.

## Level III — Ce qui doit céder

- **Premise:** on land, a tremor traps travelers behind a stone gate while a cracked roof sinks. A massive reinforcement bar locks the only exit.
- **Escalation role:** practical mastery; the player must choose the correct structural weakness instead of overpowering everything.
- **Beat sheet:**
  1. Root — gate down, roof descending, reinforcement bar blocking the exit.
  2. i01 — vibrations reveal that careless blows could finish the collapse.
  3. i02 — a trapped child raises the human stakes inside the shelter.
  4. i03 — two stone anchors carry different parts of the load.
  5. i04 — Haki is applied to the cracked socket, not the intact bar.
  6. i05 — the temporary roof support starts to fail.
  7. i06 — the hanging bar must fall into a cleared safe zone.
  8. i07 — one committed strike is aimed at the exact anchor.
  9. i08 — the exit opens because the right weakness yields and the rest remains standing.
- **Mastered:** concentrated Armament, force routing, choosing the target that should yield.
- **Choice themes:** structural reading, rescue, restraint, precision, command under pressure.
- **Risk:** serious; bad decisions can cause heavy Health loss while still preserving progression for survivors.
- **Secondary rewards:** Reputation, Morale, Charisma, Luck, minor resource expenditure.
- **Thread connection:** makes the recurring principle explicit — victory is choosing what must yield, not maximizing force.

## Level IV — Plus dur que toi

- **Premise:** at sea aboard the player’s ship, a heavily armored scavenger boards with a spring-maul that turns careless blocks into punishing rebounds.
- **Escalation role:** expertise; timing, defense, patience, protection of the helm, and one precise commitment matter more than hardness contests.
- **Beat sheet:**
  1. Root — armored boarder arrives; first maul impact reveals the rebound mechanism.
  2. i01 — overlapping plates deny clean targets.
  3. i02 — the maul’s second beat punishes single-impact guards.
  4. i03 — every careless attack opens an immediate counter.
  5. i04 — the opponent threatens the helm, forcing protective commitment.
  6. i05 — the player must wait rather than attack a closed defense.
  7. i06 — a real opening appears only while accepting simultaneous danger.
  8. i07 — one exact timing window decides the fight.
  9. i08 — the player controls when the confrontation ends; superior hardness no longer decides it.
- **Mastered:** sustained guard, selective coating, timing, patience, and commitment only when the opening exists.
- **Choice themes:** protect the ship, bait, wait, counter, break the weapon instead of the person.
- **Risk:** high; extreme choices can inflict very heavy Health loss and can kill without blocking surviving paths.
- **Secondary rewards:** Reputation, Morale, Navigation, Luck, limited ship/material-equivalent Berry costs.
- **Thread connection:** the “line” is now temporal — the moment when the opponent must yield.

## Level V — Le coup qui ne devait pas exister

- **Premise:** on land, a stone-armored behemoth charges through a temporary camp. Its impacts are large enough to destroy shelter without direct contact.
- **Escalation role:** superior mastery; the player faces a physical impossibility and wins by propagating Armament through a pre-existing fracture line rather than by receiving a source-stat increase.
- **Beat sheet:**
  1. Root — the behemoth destroys a cart and threatens the only shelter.
  2. i01 — its stride reveals that the charge follows a predictable line.
  3. i02 — the first horn impact creates a crater and a wide shockwave.
  4. i03 — the camp must be evacuated while the beast turns for another charge.
  5. i04 — close inspection reveals layered fractures in the enormous stone horn.
  6. i05 — there is no second shelter; the next line must be redirected.
  7. i06 — the player chooses the diagonal fracture line and allocates guard/offense deliberately.
  8. i07 — the impossible impact arrives; failure can be catastrophic but still carries surviving paths onward.
  9. i08 — the fracture runs through the horn, the charge diverts, and the camp survives.
- **Mastered:** superior concentration and impact control; Armament is applied to a chosen failure path inside a colossal force.
- **Choice themes:** evacuation, responsibility, angle, restraint, accepting impact, protecting others during the strike.
- **Risk:** very high/epic; failures can inflict severe Health loss and may kill, with no plot armor.
- **Secondary rewards:** Reputation, Morale, Charisma, small emergency Berry losses.
- **Thread connection:** resolves the five-impossible motif at full scale: even the impossible has a precise thing that can yield.
