## Consequence diversity

The batch deliberately uses:

- **Reputation** for praise, scandal, notoriety and public memory; all changes are non-negative;
- **Health** only where a social performance can physically go wrong (`dance_circle`);
- **Berrys** as optional social exit/gift costs, never as the main premise;
- **Stats** sparingly, mainly on clear public success/failure;
- **History/opportunity** through mechanically neutral but narratively distinct Choices/Outcomes recorded by normal History.

Persistent NPC Relationship and Item mutation are intentionally absent. The current generic-land surface has no location-safe generic persistent social NPC or neutral social Item that can be modified without creating or inappropriately consuming another track's definition. Forcing those mechanics would violate the no-definition-proliferation and broad-generic constraints.
