## Origin Cross indispensability audit

### Human × civilian — uninspected civilian passage

At a neighborhood barrier, a Fish-Man neighbor entrusts a closed provisions basket to the player because the guard waves a Human child from a civilian household through while searching visibly non-Human families. Human changes who receives automatic passage; `civilian` changes why the player reads as an ordinary local household rather than crew/faction traffic. Remove Human and the uninspected proxy disappears; remove civilian and the neutral-household passage premise changes materially.

This is not a Human-default object/interface scene: the player's Human body changes **who gets searched at a civilian checkpoint**, not tool fit, scale, clothing, controls or physical technique.

### Fish-Man × civilian — resident mistaken for transient crew

A neighborhood guard marks the Fish-Man child as a visitor even though the civilian household has lived there for years. Fish-Man changes who is singled out and why; civilian changes the contradiction, because the stake is recognition as a settled local household rather than crew/faction processing. Remove either axis and the false classification loses its engine.

The scene uses no water, aquatic task, strength expectation, Fish-Man Karate, morphology subsystem or swimming/respiration mechanic.

### Mink × civilian — boarding-house blame

A boarding-house keeper blames the Mink household for fibers left after a commercial delivery and withholds the shared meal until the mess is resolved. Mink changes the accusation and evidence problem; civilian changes the stake into shared tenancy/household responsibility rather than ship, barracks or faction quarters. Remove either axis and the scene becomes a different dispute.

The player does not track by instinct or scent. Evidence is ordinary visible wool, delivery sequence and a torn blanket.

### Giant × civilian — child taxed as an adult

A local collector classifies the Giant child as an adult because of visible size and charges the civilian household accordingly. Giant changes the classification; civilian changes the immediate stake into household taxation rather than organizational provisioning/dues. Remove either axis and the premise changes materially.

The scene never equates Giant with strength, stupidity or slowness and introduces no SizeSystem; body scale only triggers an authored social misclassification.
