# NARRATIVE_BLUEPRINT — Le Bruit derrière le monde

Five portable land-based mini-adventures form one progression: a warning arrives before evidence, becomes readable intent, survives sensory loss, is tested by deliberate deception, then expands into a multi-presence field during an epic cascade. The content never claims literal future sight.

## Level 1 — Une seconde trop tôt / One Second Too Early

**Premise.** Dans un camp de passage, une série d’accidents révèle un avertissement sensoriel qui précède les preuves ordinaires.

**Escalation role.** Éveil : passer de réactions impossibles mais instinctives à un premier acte volontaire fondé sur la perception.

**9-scene beat sheet.**
- Root — A suspended load feels wrong before any physical warning; the player reacts around a child in its path.
- i01 — The rope actually breaks and the swinging load creates a second moving hazard.
- i02 — A cart is sensed as about to bolt before the animal visibly panics.
- i03 — A beam is known to be falling before the crack, forcing another rescue.
- i04 — A witness asks how the player knew; the player can deliberately search for the sensory delay.
- i05 — A missing beat in the camp noise warns of a rain-heavy canopy collapse.
- i06 — A crowded footbridge becomes the next pre-visible danger.
- i07 — The player must trust the warning with no proof to save the last person on the bridge.
- i08 — The player deliberately acts before a final crack and accepts the perception as real.

**Narrative mastery.** Sentir une présence ou un danger avant les signes ordinaires et accepter de faire confiance à ce signal.

**Major Choice themes.** protection, warning without proof, physical intervention, crowd control, deliberate trust

**Risk profile.** faible/modéré.

**Secondary reward possibilities.** small Morale/Luck/Charisma or Reputation gains; modest Health/resource costs on risky failures

**Thread connection.** Introduces the recurring sensory lag: the world seems one beat behind the warning.

## Level 2 — Ce que les gestes cachent / What Gestures Hide

**Premise.** Une dispute autour d’une sacoche oblige le personnage à distinguer peur, mensonge, agression et vol avant que les gestes ne les révèlent.

**Escalation role.** Contrôle : reproduire volontairement l’état perceptif et lire l’intention plutôt que la seule action visible.

**9-scene beat sheet.**
- Root — A dispute over a stolen satchel begins with a hidden strike felt before it starts.
- i01 — A second person claims peace while preparing a concealed move.
- i02 — Fear, lying, and intent to flee must be separated from intent to steal.
- i03 — A quiet bystander reveals theft intent while everyone watches the argument.
- i04 — The player deliberately recreates the calm state instead of waiting for instinct.
- i05 — The satchel becomes bait, forcing a read across three conflicting reactions.
- i06 — Two simultaneous gestures expose the difference between a feint and the real plan.
- i07 — Several intentions coexist; the player must identify which becomes dangerous now.
- i08 — The player enters the state on purpose and catches intent before it becomes motion.

**Narrative mastery.** Percevoir une intention avant son geste, séparer signaux contradictoires et entrer volontairement dans l’état de perception.

**Major Choice themes.** deception, aggression, fear, feints, deliberate focus, intervention before motion

**Risk profile.** modéré.

**Secondary reward possibilities.** Morale/Charisma/Luck, minor Reputation, and moderate Health downside when confrontation goes wrong

**Thread connection.** Turns the same warning into readable pre-action intent and teaches deliberate access to the state.

## Level 3 — Le monde sans yeux / The World Without Eyes

**Premise.** Un atelier envahi de fumée et de débris transforme la perception en outil de navigation, de secours et de coordination sans vision fiable.

**Escalation role.** Maîtrise pratique : suivre plusieurs présences, ignorer les faux sons et agir dans un espace que les sens ordinaires ne décrivent plus correctement.

**9-scene beat sheet.**
- Root — A lamp blast fills a temporary workshop with smoke and immediately destroys reliable sight.
- i01 — The room becomes fully opaque while people and debris cross the player’s path.
- i02 — Echoing cries point the wrong way while a real trapped presence stays elsewhere.
- i03 — A blast-opened gap forces navigation among moving people without sight.
- i04 — The player must count several presences and isolate one faint missing person.
- i05 — A trapped person under a beam requires coordinated rescue inside the smoke.
- i06 — Competing passages make sound unreliable; moving presences become the route clue.
- i07 — The player leads a whole group through total smoke without depending on vision.
- i08 — During final collapse, the player tracks everyone and the opening gap in darkness.

**Narrative mastery.** Naviguer, protéger et guider sans dépendre de la vue, tout en distinguant plusieurs présences dans la confusion sensorielle.

**Major Choice themes.** blind navigation, rescue, coordination, misleading sound, presence counting, route choice

**Risk profile.** sérieux.

**Secondary reward possibilities.** Navigation/Morale/Charisma, rescue Reputation, and serious Health costs under collapse/smoke

**Thread connection.** Proves the perception survives when ordinary sight and sound become unreliable.

## Level 4 — Le silence qui ment / The Silence That Lies

**Premise.** Trois assaillants exploitent volontairement feintes, intentions interrompues, immobilité et pièges pour tromper une perception trop littérale.

**Escalation role.** Expertise : comprendre que l’Observation n’est pas omnisciente et interpréter intention, absence, terrain et engagement réel ensemble.

**9-scene beat sheet.**
- Root — Masked attackers deliberately present loud intent as bait while a second attack lands elsewhere.
- i01 — Repeated attacks stop and restart to train the player into reacting to false commitment.
- i02 — A mechanical trap demonstrates that danger can exist without current human intent.
- i03 — A hostage threat weaponizes murderous intent to move the player rather than to kill the captive.
- i04 — An attacker cancels real intent so a separate projectile can exploit fixation.
- i05 — Deliberate stillness tries to make absence of signal feel like safety.
- i06 — Three apparent threats contain only two real commitments, forcing instant prioritization.
- i07 — The calmest attacker suppresses outward intent and must be read through context and forced commitment.
- i08 — The player integrates intent, terrain, timing, feints and uncertainty instead of treating Observation as omniscience.

**Narrative mastery.** Résister aux feintes et au silence fabriqué, reconnaître les dangers sans intention et lire le contexte au-delà du signal brut.

**Major Choice themes.** feint resistance, commitment, uncertainty, terrain awareness, hostage pressure, controlled opponents

**Risk profile.** élevé.

**Secondary reward possibilities.** Morale/Charisma/Luck and Reputation; high Health losses on misreads and extreme feints

**Thread connection.** Breaks naive confidence by showing that intent can be feigned, canceled, hidden, or absent from mechanical danger.

## Level 5 — Avant que le monde bouge / Before the World Moves

**Premise.** Un désastre soudain transforme un vaste camp en système chaotique de foule, feu, pillards, animaux, terrain qui cède et dangers en cascade.

**Escalation role.** Maîtrise supérieure : lire simultanément de nombreuses présences et agir sur la chaîne causale juste avant ses confirmations visibles, sans prétendre voir littéralement le futur.

**9-scene beat sheet.**
- Root — A sudden ground shock turns a large temporary camp into a field of dozens of diverging presences and imminent structural failures.
- i01 — A falling mast and sliding cart create crossing trajectories around one fallen person.
- i02 — Fire and panic threaten to funnel three groups into one deadly exit.
- i03 — A small still presence must be found inside a moving crowd before the crowd collides with it.
- i04 — Raiders exploit the chaos, and hostile intent must be isolated from mass flight intent.
- i05 — A ground fissure cannot be sensed directly, but the human trajectories it threatens can be read.
- i06 — A debris slope begins to fail and the evacuation must be conducted as one moving system.
- i07 — The whole cascade becomes a sequence of irreversible moments that must be prioritized one breath early.
- i08 — The climax holds the entire multi-presence field and acts before visible confirmation without claiming literal future sight.

**Narrative mastery.** Maintenir un champ multi-présence, hiérarchiser des dangers en cascade et agir un souffle avant le monde visible.

**Major Choice themes.** triage, crowd flow, cascading hazards, hostile intent inside panic, evacuation, whole-field prioritization

**Risk profile.** très élevé/épique.

**Secondary reward possibilities.** Morale/Navigation/Charisma/Luck and major Reputation; very high Health costs for failed epic interventions

**Thread connection.** Combines every lesson into a whole-field read of many presences and cascading consequences, producing the “before the world moves” feeling without literal prophecy.
